About us

Jangbadisaparati.ge — oxygen concentrator rental and sales in Tbilisi

We rent and sell oxygen concentrators — 5 and 10-liter devices — with delivery, installation and 24/7 technical support.

Call Message on WhatsApp

Who we are

Jangbadisaparati.ge is a service based in Tbilisi that rents and sells oxygen concentrators. Our aim is to make it simple for a person whose doctor has prescribed oxygen therapy to get the device they need, and to be available for technical questions when needed.

What we offer

  • Rental: 5-liter — 10 GEL per day; 10-liter — 15 GEL per day
  • Sales: 5-liter — 1,300 GEL; 10-liter — 2,200 GEL; warranty — 6 months
  • On-site delivery in Tbilisi and installation
  • 24/7 technical support
  • Cleaning and sterilization of the device before rental

How we prepare a rental device

Before rental the device goes through cleaning and sterilization. It comes with a pulse oximeter (saturation meter), cannula, mask, distilled water and the necessary accessories.

Where to find us

Our address: Building 1, Vazisubani Settlement, Tbilisi 0153, Georgia. Open in Google Maps or see the contact page.

Important note

Important: use of an oxygen concentrator, oxygen flow rate and duration of use must be determined according to the treating physician’s recommendation.

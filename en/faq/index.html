Oxygen concentrators — general questions

It is a medical device that separates nitrogen from room air and delivers high-concentration oxygen.
Depending on the mode and model, the oxygen concentration delivered can be approximately 90–95%.
Unlike a cylinder, a concentrator does not need periodic refilling — it runs on electricity and can provide a continuous oxygen supply.
The appropriate device and oxygen flow parameters should be selected according to your doctor’s recommendation.
The devices are designed for long-term continuous operation.

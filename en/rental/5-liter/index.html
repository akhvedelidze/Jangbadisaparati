How rental works

  1. Call us (592 15 54 99 / 544 44 12 40) or message us on WhatsApp.
  2. Tell us which size you need (choose the size on your doctor’s recommendation) and for how long.
  3. We agree the delivery time and your address in Tbilisi.
  4. We deliver and install the device, hand over the accessories and explain how to use it.
  5. During use, 24/7 technical support is available for technical questions.

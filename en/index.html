Information

What is an oxygen concentrator?

An oxygen concentrator is a medical device that separates nitrogen from room air and delivers high-concentration oxygen.

Unlike an oxygen cylinder, a concentrator does not need periodic refilling — it runs on electricity and can provide a continuous oxygen supply.

Depending on the mode and model, the oxygen concentration delivered can be approximately 90–95%.

Important: use of an oxygen concentrator, oxygen flow rate and duration of use must be determined according to the treating physician’s recommendation.

What is an oxygen concentrator?

Verified information from Jangbadisaparati.ge

  • A concentrator separates nitrogen from room air and delivers high-concentration oxygen.
  • It runs on electricity and, unlike a cylinder, does not need periodic refilling; it can provide a continuous oxygen supply.
  • Depending on the mode and model, the oxygen concentration can be approximately 90–95%.
